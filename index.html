Rules and assumptions36 credits · 7 electives · 2 outside maximum
  • Degree total: 36 credits: 3 mandatory research-methodology credits, 21 elective credits, and 12 thesis credits.
  • Specialization choice: seven Data Science electives; up to two may come from the other specialization, general courses, or approved Business School courses.
  • Semester model: BRAC's programme page lists a three-semester duration. This board keeps your four-semester planning model, with a 6-credit first term and 12-credit later terms; confirm which schedule applies to you.
  • Thesis: choose one 12-credit block or split the thesis into CSE700A and CSE700B. CSE791 must precede CSE700B.
  • Course advice: course order, ratings, topic coverage, and workload comments are recommendations, not official prerequisites or assessment data.
